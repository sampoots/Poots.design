Hi, I'm Sam. I build websites the way I climb: carefully.

Where I'm coming from

Engineering, the mountains, my team and my charity work have all shaped how I work.

⚙️Engineering

Manufacturing engineering is about building things to a spec, measuring them, testing them, and getting them right before they leave the workshop.

I bring that to every website. Each one is planned, built to a clear brief, and tested on real phones before it goes live.

🧗Climbing & mountaineering

On a climb or a mountain day, you plan your route, check your kit, and look after the people you're with. Cutting corners isn't an option.

That's how I run projects: a clear plan, honest updates, and finishing what I start.

🏈Belfast Trojans

I've played for the Belfast Trojans youth team for three years. Turning up to every training session, learning the plays and doing your job so the team can do theirs.

I work with clients the same way: as a team, with clear roles, good communication and the same goal.

🐾Charity work

I volunteer with Rescue Cats NI, a cat rescue charity, and was one of the people who helped rebuild the roof at their rescue.

It taught me that good work is about the people it helps. Most of my clients are small, local and doing something they care about, and I want to help them do it well.
