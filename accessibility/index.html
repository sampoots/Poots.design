What I aim for

This site aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA, and I build client sites to the same standard.

What I've done

  • Text and buttons are checked for colour contrast.
  • Everything can be used with a keyboard, with a visible focus outline.
  • Animations switch off if your device is set to "reduce motion".
  • Forms have proper labels and clear error messages.
  • Pages work when zoomed to 200% and on small phone screens.
  • Decorative graphics are hidden from screen readers; meaningful ones are described.

Known limitations

  • The example sites are design samples and haven't had a full accessibility audit.
  • Enquiry forms are sent through Formspree, a third-party service.
  • This site hasn't yet been tested by an independent accessibility specialist.

Found a problem?

If something is hard to use, please tell me and I'll fix it or send you the information another way: 07395 229191 or infopootsdesign@gmail.com. I'll reply within 5 working days.
