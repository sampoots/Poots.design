Privacy notice

Last updated 12 October 2026

In short: I only collect what you send me, I only use it to reply to you and do the work you ask for, I never sell it, and this website doesn't use cookies or tracking.

Who I am

Poots Design is a trading name of Sam Poots, a sole trader based in Northern Ireland. I'm responsible for any personal information you give me. You can contact me at pootssam5@gmail.com or on 07395 229191.

What I collect

Only what you choose to send me through the enquiry form, by email, by phone or by message:

  • your name and business name
  • your phone number and/or email address
  • what you tell me about your business and the website you need

If you become a client, I also keep the records needed to do the work and to invoice you.

Why I use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and send you a mock-up or quote. This is in our legitimate interests, and it's a step you've asked for before any agreement.
  • To build and look after your website if you become a client. This is needed to carry out our agreement.
  • To keep business and tax records as the law requires.

I won't add you to a mailing list or send you marketing you haven't asked for.

Who else handles it

  • Formspree receives messages sent through the enquiry form and forwards them to my email. Formspree is based in the United States, and transfers are covered by their data protection terms.
  • Google (Gmail) stores my email.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website. Like most web hosts, it may log visitors' IP addresses for security.

I never sell or share your details with anyone else for marketing.

How long I keep it

  • Enquiries that don't go ahead: deleted within 12 months.
  • Client records and invoices: kept for 6 years for tax purposes, then deleted.

Cookies

This website doesn't use cookies, analytics or tracking.

Your rights

You can ask me to show you, correct or delete the information I hold about you, or to stop using it. Just email or call me, and I'll respond within one month.

If you're unhappy with how I've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113. I'd appreciate the chance to sort it out first.
