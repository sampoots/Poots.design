Terms of business

The plain-English terms for working with Poots Design. Last updated 13 October 2026.

In short: you get a fixed price before anything starts. 50% deposit to begin, the rest before your site goes live. You own your site once it's paid for in full. Please send your content and replies promptly, make sure you have the right to use what you send me, and talk to me first if anything goes wrong. If you're a consumer, nothing here takes away your legal rights.

1. Who I am and how these terms work

  • Poots Design is a trading name of Sam Poots, a sole trader based in Belfast (BT7), Northern Ireland. Contact: 07395 229191 or infopootsdesign@gmail.com. My full business address is on every quote and invoice.
  • These terms apply to every job I do. Your written quote and these terms together make up our agreement. If the quote and these terms disagree, the quote wins.
  • You accept these terms when you accept a quote in writing (an email or message is fine), pay a deposit, or ask me to start work, whichever happens first.
  • Some parts apply only to business clients (you're buying for your trade, business or organisation) or only to consumers (you're buying for personal use). Where that's the case, it says so.

2. Quotes and prices

  • Every job starts with a written quote showing exactly what's included and the fixed price.
  • Quotes are valid for 30 days.
  • I'm not VAT registered, so no VAT is added. If that changes, I'll tell you before it affects any price.
  • Anything outside the quote is priced and agreed with you before I do it. There are no surprise charges.
  • Prices on my website are for guidance. The price that applies to your job is the one on your quote.

3. Deposits and payment

  • A 50% deposit books your start date. I don't start work until it's paid.
  • The remaining 50% is due when I show you the finished site, before it goes live. Your site won't be launched or handed over until it's paid in full.
  • Monthly services, like the care plan, are paid monthly in advance.
  • Invoices are due within 14 days unless your invoice says otherwise.
  • You can pay by bank transfer, or by card through a secure payment link. Card payments appear on your statement as POOTS GROUP. I never ask for card details by phone, email or text.

4. Late or missed payments

  • If a payment is late, I'll remind you first. Mistakes happen and I'd rather sort it out with a quick message.
  • If an invoice is still unpaid 14 days after it was due, I may pause work on your project until it's paid. Any agreed timeline moves by the length of the pause.
  • If an invoice for a live site or care plan is still unpaid after that, I'll give you at least 7 days' written notice, and then I may suspend the services I provide (such as hosting and changes), which can mean taking the site offline, until the account is paid. I'll restore it promptly once it's paid.
  • Business clients: I may claim interest and fixed compensation on late payments under the Late Payment of Commercial Debts (Interest) Act 1998, and the reasonable costs of recovering the debt.

5. What's included, changes and sign-off

  • Your package includes the pages, features and rounds of changes shown on your quote.
  • A "round of changes" is one list of changes sent together. Extra rounds, extra pages or new features are quoted first (extra pages from £60).
  • When I've made the changes and you confirm you're happy, or you ask me to put the site live, that counts as sign-off. Changes after sign-off are new work and are quoted separately, or covered by the care plan.
  • If something I built doesn't work as described, tell me within 30 days of going live and I'll fix it free of charge. This doesn't limit your legal rights.
  • I'll build your site to work on current versions of the main web browsers (Chrome, Safari, Edge and Firefox) on phones and computers. I can't guarantee how it looks on very old or unusual browsers.

6. Your responsibilities

  • Your content. You'll supply your logo, photos, wording, prices and business details. The start your project page lists what I need.
  • Rights to use it. You confirm you own, or have permission to use, everything you send me, such as photos, logos, reviews and text. Please don't send images copied from Google or other websites.
  • Accuracy and legality. You're responsible for making sure what's on your site is accurate and lawful: for example prices, opening hours, qualifications, insurance, allergen and food information, and any claims about your business. I'll flag anything that looks wrong, but I can't check it all for you.
  • Your own legal duties. If your business has rules to follow (for example data protection, consumer law, trade body rules or advertising rules), following them on your site is your responsibility.
  • Replying. Please reply to questions and send content within a reasonable time, so your project keeps moving.
  • Business clients: if someone makes a claim against me because of content you supplied or asked me to publish (for example a photo you didn't have the rights to, or a misleading claim), you agree to cover my reasonable costs and losses from that claim.

7. Delays and going quiet

  • Delivery times (for example "about 5 working days") start once I have your deposit and your content. If content or replies arrive late, the timeline moves with them.
  • If I'm waiting on you for more than 30 days, I may pause your project and move on to other work. When you're ready, I'll restart as soon as my schedule allows.
  • If I've had no reply for 60 days, after at least two reminders by email, I may treat the project as finished. I'll send you what I've built so far and invoice the remaining balance for the work done. You'll never be charged for work I haven't done.

8. Who owns what

  • Once you've paid in full, you own the design and content made specifically for you, and I give you a permanent licence to use any of my general code needed to run your site.
  • Until you've paid in full, I own the work, and you can't publish, copy or pass it to someone else to finish.
  • Your content, logo and branding are always yours.
  • I keep the right to reuse my general building blocks (code, layouts and templates) for other clients, but never your content or branding.
  • Some parts of your site may come from other people, such as fonts, icons, stock photos or booking widgets. They're used under their own licences, which I'll make sure allow your use.
  • I may add a small "Website by Poots Design" link in your site's footer. Just ask and I'll remove it at no charge.
  • I'll only show your site in my portfolio or use your name in marketing if you say yes.

9. Other providers, domains and accounts

  • Domains, email, hosting, booking systems, payment providers and social media are run by separate companies with their own terms and fees. You sign up to them directly and the accounts are in your name, so you stay in control. I set things up for you.
  • Those companies can change their prices, features or terms, or have outages. I'm not responsible for their services, their charges or changes they make, but I'll help you work around problems where I can.
  • Free hosting. I set your website up on a free hosting service, in an account in your name where possible, so there's no monthly hosting fee. Free plans are provided by those companies, not by me. If a provider ever changes or ends its free plan, I'll tell you your options, and any new hosting cost would be yours.
  • Your domain name. Renewing your domain on time is your responsibility. If it lapses, someone else could buy it. I recommend turning on auto-renew.
  • Passwords. I'll never ask for your passwords. Where I need access, you'll add me as a user or manager, and you can remove me at any time.

10. Hosting, security and backups

  • I take reasonable steps to keep the sites I build and host secure and online, such as using simple, fast technology with fewer weak points, keeping a security certificate in place, and keeping backups.
  • No website or host can be guaranteed to be 100% secure or online all the time. I can't be responsible for outages, attacks or data loss caused by hosting companies or other providers, or by someone else getting into your accounts.
  • Please keep your own copies of your photos, wording and other content. After launch, I'll send you a copy of your site files on request.

11. Not legal or professional advice

I can point out things you might want to check, like adding a privacy notice or showing your business details. But I'm a web designer, not a lawyer, accountant or other professional adviser, and nothing I say is legal, tax or financial advice. Check anything important with a qualified adviser.

12. Care plan

  • £25 a month, paid in advance: I look after your hosting account and security certificate, take backups, check your site is online, and make up to 30 minutes of changes a month. Unused minutes don't roll over.
  • The care plan is optional. Your site's hosting doesn't depend on it.
  • Bigger changes, new pages or new features aren't included. I'll quote them first and never add them without asking.
  • Request changes through the care plan changes form.
  • Cancel any time before your next monthly payment. There's no minimum term.
  • If I need to change the price, I'll give you at least 30 days' notice so you can cancel first if you want to.
  • When you leave, and your account is paid up, I'll hand over your site files and help you move them.

13. Cancelling

If you cancel

  • If you cancel before I start work, your deposit is refunded in full.
  • If work has started, you pay for the work done up to that point (based on the stage reached and time spent), and I refund anything you've paid above that. If the work done is worth more than the deposit, the difference is due.
  • Consumers: you have a legal right to cancel within 14 days of agreeing to the work, for any reason. If you ask me to start within those 14 days and then cancel, you'll pay for the work done up to the point you told me. Email or message me to cancel.

If I need to end our agreement

  • I may end our agreement by telling you in writing if: payments are seriously overdue after reminders; you're abusive, threatening or harassing towards me; you ask me to publish something unlawful, harmful or misleading; or you seriously break these terms.
  • If I end it for any of those reasons, you pay for the work done so far. If I end it for any other reason, I'll refund everything you've paid for work I haven't done.

14. Payment disputes and chargebacks

  • If you're unhappy with anything, please tell me first. I'll reply within 5 working days and do my best to put it right.
  • Please don't raise a chargeback or payment dispute with your bank for work I've delivered without contacting me first. If you do, I may pause or suspend work and services on your account while it's resolved, and I'll send your bank evidence of the work done and of our agreement.
  • Business clients: if a chargeback is found in my favour, you'll also cover the fee the payment provider charged me.
  • This doesn't affect your legal rights, including your right to dispute a payment with your bank.

15. Data your website collects

  • If your website collects personal information from your customers (for example through an enquiry or booking form), you're the controller of that information, and I act as your processor where I handle it for you.
  • As your processor, I'll only use that information to provide my services and on your instructions, keep it confidential, take reasonable steps to keep it secure, and only share it with the service providers needed to run your site (for example form and hosting providers). I'll tell you without delay if I become aware of a data breach affecting it, help you respond to requests from your customers, and delete or return it at the end of our agreement unless the law requires me to keep it.
  • You're responsible for having a lawful reason to collect that information and for your own privacy notice. I can add one to your site if you give me the wording.
  • How I handle your personal information is explained in my privacy notice.

16. Responsibility and liability

  • I'll do the work with reasonable care and skill.
  • I can't guarantee search rankings, a number of visitors or enquiries, or any increase in sales, because those depend on many things outside my control.
  • I'm not responsible for problems caused by: content you supplied; changes you or someone else makes to the site, its hosting or its accounts; or failures of other providers.
  • Business clients: I'm not liable for indirect or consequential losses, or for loss of profit, revenue, business, data or goodwill. My total liability to you for any job is limited to the amount you've paid me for that job (for the care plan, the total paid in the previous 12 months).
  • Consumers: I'm responsible for loss or damage you suffer that is a foreseeable result of me breaking these terms or not using reasonable care and skill. I'm not responsible for business losses.
  • Nothing in these terms limits liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, for fraud, or for anything else the law doesn't allow to be limited. Nothing in these terms affects your legal rights as a consumer.

17. Events outside my control

I'm not responsible for delays caused by things outside my reasonable control, such as serious illness, a family emergency, power or internet failures, or problems at hosting and other providers. I'll let you know as soon as I can and agree a new timeline with you. If the delay lasts more than 30 days, either of us can cancel, and I'll refund anything you've paid for work I haven't done.

18. General

  • Who does the work. Poots Design is a one-person business and I do the work myself, using software tools to help. If a job ever needs a specialist I don't have the skills for, I'll ask you first. I stay responsible for the work and for keeping your information confidential.
  • Confidentiality. I'll keep your business information private, and only use it to do the work you've asked for.
  • Notices. Notices under these terms can be sent by email to the address you gave me, or to infopootsdesign@gmail.com.
  • If part of these terms doesn't apply. If a court decides any part of these terms can't be enforced, the rest still applies.
  • Not enforcing straight away. If I don't enforce a right immediately, I can still do so later.
  • Transfer. You can't transfer this agreement to someone else without my agreement.
  • Changes to these terms. I may update these terms from time to time. The version in place on your quote date applies to your job, and care plan clients will get 30 days' notice of changes that affect them.
  • Law. These terms are governed by the law of Northern Ireland, and disputes are handled by the courts of Northern Ireland. If you're a consumer living elsewhere in the UK, you can also bring a claim in your local courts.
