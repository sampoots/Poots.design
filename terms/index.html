Terms of business

The plain-English terms for working with Poots Design. Last updated 13 October 2026.

In short: you get a fixed price before anything starts. 50% deposit to begin, the rest before your site goes live. You own your site and content once it's paid for. If you cancel before I start, you get your deposit back.

1. Who I am

Poots Design is a trading name of Sam Poots, a sole trader based in Belfast (BT7), Northern Ireland. Contact: 07395 229191 or pootssam5@gmail.com. My full business address is on every quote and invoice.

2. Quotes and prices

  • Every job starts with a written quote showing exactly what's included and the fixed price.
  • Quotes are valid for 30 days.
  • I'm not VAT registered, so no VAT is added.
  • Anything outside the quote is priced and agreed with you before I do it. There are no surprise charges.

3. Deposits and payment

  • A 50% deposit secures your start date. The balance is due before your site goes live.
  • Monthly services (like the care plan) are paid monthly in advance.
  • You can pay by bank transfer, or by card through a secure payment link. Card payments appear on your statement as POOTS GROUP.
  • Invoices are due within 14 days. Your site won't be launched until it's paid in full.
  • For business clients, I may charge interest and compensation on late payments, as allowed by law.

4. What's included and changes

  • Your package includes the number of pages and rounds of changes shown on your quote.
  • A "round of changes" is one list of changes sent together. Extra rounds or extra pages are quoted first (extra pages from £60).
  • After launch, changes are covered by the care plan, or quoted individually.

5. Your content

  • You'll supply your logo, photos, wording, prices and business details. The project checklist lists what I need.
  • You confirm you have the right to use anything you send me (for example, photos you took or paid for).
  • You're responsible for your details being accurate and legal, such as prices, opening hours and any claims about your business. I'll flag anything that looks wrong.

6. Timelines

Delivery times (for example "about a week") start once I have your deposit and your content. If content arrives late, the timeline moves with it.

7. Who owns what

  • Once you've paid in full, you own the website made for you and all your content.
  • Your domain name is registered in your name, so it's always yours.
  • I keep the right to reuse my general building blocks (code, layouts and templates), but never your content or branding.
  • I'll only show your site in my portfolio if you say yes.

8. Other providers

Domains, email, hosting, booking systems and payment providers are separate companies with their own terms and fees. You sign up to them directly, and I set things up for you. I'm not responsible for their outages or their charges.

9. Care plan

  • £35 a month: hosting, security certificate, backups, uptime checks and up to 30 minutes of changes a month. Unused minutes don't roll over.
  • Request changes through the care plan changes form.
  • Cancel any time before your next monthly payment. If you leave, I'll hand over your site files and help you move them.

10. Cancelling

  • If you cancel before work starts, your deposit is refunded in full.
  • If work has started, I'll keep a fair amount for the work done so far and refund the rest.
  • If you're buying as a consumer (not for a business) you may also have a legal right to cancel within 14 days. If you ask me to start within those 14 days, you may have to pay for work already done.

11. Responsibility

  • I'll do the work with reasonable care and skill.
  • I can't guarantee search rankings or a particular number of enquiries, because those depend on things outside my control.
  • For business clients, I'm not liable for indirect losses such as lost profits, and my total liability is limited to the amount you've paid me for the job.
  • Nothing in these terms affects your legal rights as a consumer.

12. Your information

How I handle personal information is explained in the privacy notice.

13. General

These terms, together with your quote, are the whole agreement. If they ever change, the version on your quote date applies to your job. These terms are governed by the law of Northern Ireland, and disputes are handled by the courts of Northern Ireland.
