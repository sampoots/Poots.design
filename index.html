Questions people ask

If yours isn't here, just ask.

How long does it take?

A Starter site is usually live in about 5 working days, and a Business site in 7 to 10, once I have your details and photos.

What do I need to give you?

Your business name, what you offer, the areas you cover, how customers should contact you, and any photos or logo you have. If you don't have photos or wording, I'll help you put them together.

Do I own the website?

Yes. Your domain name and all your content belong to you. If you ever leave, I'll hand everything over.

Can I make changes after it's live?

Yes. On the care plan, up to 30 minutes of changes a month are included. Without it, small changes are £60 each.

I already have a domain or an old website. Is that a problem?

Not at all. I can use your existing domain, replace or refresh your old site, and make sure nothing breaks when it switches over.

Are there any ongoing costs?

Your domain name, usually around £10 to £15 a year, paid directly to the provider so it stays in your name. The care plan is optional at £35 a month, and you can cancel any time.

Can my site take payments or food orders?

Yes. I can add card payments for deposits and invoices, or online ordering for food businesses, using trusted providers like Stripe, SumUp, Square or Flipdish. You sign up with the provider and pay their small per-transaction fee; card details never touch your website.

How do I pay?

50% to start and 50% before the site goes live. You'll get a written quote first, so you know the full cost up front.
