Prices & payment

How do I pay?

50% to start and 50% before the site goes live. You'll get a written quote first, so you know the full cost up front.

Are there any ongoing costs?

Your domain name, usually around £10 to £15 a year, paid directly to the provider so it stays in your name. Hosting is free, so there's no monthly fee. The care plan is optional at £25 a month, with no contract.

Why are your prices lower than many other web designers?

I'm a one-person business with low overheads: no office, sales team or account managers to pay for. My Launch and Essential packages use templates built for your trade, and every site is built to be simple and fast, which means it can be hosted for free. You still get a fixed price, a free mock-up before you pay, and a site you own.

Can I make changes after it's live?

Yes. On the care plan, up to 30 minutes of changes a month are included. Without it, small changes are £60 each.

Can I update the site myself?

Most owners just send me a message. On the care plan, up to 30 minutes of changes a month are included; without it, small changes are £60 each. If editing it yourself matters to you, tell me before we start and I'll tell you honestly what's possible.

The process

How long does it take?

A Launch site is usually live in about 3 working days, Essential and Starter in about 5 to 7, and Business in 7 to 10, once I have your details and photos.

What's the difference between Launch, Essential and Starter?

Launch and Essential use a ready-made template for your trade with your own logo, photos and text, which keeps the price down. Starter is designed around your brand, and I write and polish the wording for you.

What do I need to give you?

Your business name, what you offer, the areas you cover, how customers should contact you, and any photos or logo you have. If you don't have photos or wording, I'll help you put them together.

I already have a domain or an old website. Is that a problem?

Not at all. I can use your existing domain, replace or refresh your old site, and make sure nothing breaks when it switches over.

What if I don't like the mock-up?

Then you owe nothing. The mock-up is free and there's no obligation to go ahead.

Will I be top of Google?

Nobody can honestly promise that. I build your pages around your services and the towns you cover, and set up the basics Google looks for, so local searches can find you.

Ownership & support

Do I own the website?

Yes. Your domain name and all your content belong to you. If you ever leave, I'll hand everything over.

Will someone else build my site?

No. I design and build every site myself, and I'm the one you talk to throughout. I use good software tools to work efficiently, but there's no outsourcing and no hand-offs.

You're a one-person business. What if you're ill or away?

Your site keeps running on its own: it's on reliable hosting, it's backed up, and you own your domain and files. I'll tell you in advance about any planned time off. If I ever couldn't finish your project, you'd get everything I'd built so far and a refund for any work not done. That's written into my terms.

Can my site take payments or food orders?

Yes. I can add card payments for deposits and invoices, or online ordering for food businesses, using trusted providers like Stripe, SumUp, Square or Flipdish. You sign up with the provider and pay their small per-transaction fee; card details never touch your website.
